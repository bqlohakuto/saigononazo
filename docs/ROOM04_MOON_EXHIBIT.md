# Room 04 moon exhibit

Uses the accepted original artwork `世界の月模様図鑑.png` (1536 × 1024). `images/exhibits/room04/moon-views-atlas.webp` is a lossless re-encoding; decoded RGBA pixels were verified identical.

The artwork is unchanged. SVG viewBox windows show only the existing moon illustrations. Captions are separate UI: a small national flag followed by the English country name, with no Japanese country label or explanatory prose.

The seven illustrated countries are ITALY, JAPAN, BELGIUM, POLAND, THAILAND, CHINA and SPAIN. The China flag is another window into the original artwork. The Spain flag retains the complete emblem-bearing SVG used to calculate the puzzle color areas.

Windows (x, y, width, height):

- ITALY: 1300, 302, 160, 160
- JAPAN: 77, 302, 160, 160
- BELGIUM: 76, 636, 160, 160
- POLAND: 810, 302, 160, 160
- THAILAND: 566, 302, 160, 160
- CHINA: 321, 302, 160, 160
- SPAIN: 1056, 302, 160, 160

These are the author-approved illustrated interpretations, not a claim that every resident of a country shares a single tradition.
